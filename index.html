The halls

    How to visit

    1. Tap Enter the museum and allow the camera.
    2. Point your phone at an exhibit frame on the wall, from about 40–80 cm away.
    3. Watch the exhibit come alive, read the plaque, or tap Audio guide.
    4. Visit all six to complete your museum passport.

    No printed frames? Choose Museum around you: stand up, hold your phone in front of you and turn slowly. Each exhibit comes alive when it is in front of you.
